# T012 — Pointer drag, cancellation and autoscroll

- Milestone: P6
- Dependencies: T011
- Model suitability (advisory, not assignment): Codex / Grok / other model with browser/pointer testing tools.
- Status: see [TASKS.md](../TASKS.md); only the coordinator assigns this task.

## Scope

Implement mouse drag and one-gesture touch drag from face-up cards with capture, 6px threshold, rAF overlay, legal hit testing, cancellation/click suppression and edge autoscroll. Preserve pinch zoom and vertical scrolling from open tableau space and face-down cards; keep scroll buttons available. Physical acceptance is T013.
