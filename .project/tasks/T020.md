## Acceptance criteria

New worker waits during play/drag; only accepted safe saved reload activates. Save failure blocks reload without losing progress. Interrupted install/update and offline failure preserve old play. Cache cleanup preserves sibling app and documented previous generation. Android/iPhone standalone/install evidence recorded or BLOCKED.

Acceptance reference: P9 lifecycle subset; TEST_PLAN T23 and §5 (PWA).
