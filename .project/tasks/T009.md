## Acceptance criteria

All seven columns fit every specified viewport, no horizontal overflow. Card ratio/14px rank/24px face-up band and control targets meet the contract. Long column is reachable with top/bottom controls; waste never covers foundations. Hidden identities absent from markup. Recalculate on resize; do not claim interactive P5 complete.

Acceptance reference: P5 layout subset; TEST_PLAN T17/T19 (layout).
