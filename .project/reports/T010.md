## Work completed

- Implemented `src/ui/input.js`:
  - `createInputController`: wires DOM click and keyboard events using event delegation on `rootElement`.
  - Tap selection and move precedence matching F01:
    - Tapping stock draws a card or recycles waste when stock is empty.
    - Tapping a movable source card selects it; legal destinations are highlighted.
    - If a source is selected, tapping a legal destination executes the move immediately ("destination interpretation takes priority when legal").
    - Retapping the same card deselects it; tapping blank background deselects it.
    - Tapping another movable card reselects that card as the new source.
    - Tapping an invalid destination leaves selection and board intact and triggers rejection feedback (shake animation and Polish announcement).
  - Pure engine validation: uses `validateMove(state, command)` from `src/engine/game.js` directly; no DOM-derived game rules.
  - History controls: click handlers on Undo ("Cofnij") and Redo ("Ponów") buttons.
  - Keyboard navigation (N01 / ARCHITECTURE §9):
    - Arrow keys navigate across piles and exposed cards:
      - Upper piles (Stock, Waste, Foundations 0–3) traversed left/right in visual order.
      - ArrowDown from upper pile enters matching tableau column at its first exposed card.
      - ArrowUp from first exposed card enters nearest upper pile.
      - ArrowUp/ArrowDown in tableau navigates along exposed cards.
      - ArrowLeft/ArrowRight in tableau navigates to nearest exposed card by vertical position in adjacent column.
      - ArrowDown at column bottom navigates to bottom bar controls.
    - Space key on focused stock draws a card.
    - Enter and Space select cards and execute legal moves.
    - Escape clears active card selection.
    - Shortcuts: Ctrl/Cmd+Z executes Undo; Ctrl/Cmd+Y and Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z execute Redo. Shortcuts ignore text input fields (`INPUT`, `TEXTAREA`).
    - Tab key naturally navigates controls and leaves the board without focus trapping.
    - Focus survival: visible focus is preserved on destination / stock after moves, draws, recycles, and Undo/Redo actions.
  - Polish live announcements (`#live-announcer`, role="status", aria-live="polite", aria-atomic="true"):
    - Selection ("Zaznaczono... Wybierz miejsce docelowe.").
    - Move execution and auto-reveals ("Wykonano ruch do... Odkryto nową kartę: ...").
    - Stock actions ("Dobrano kartę ze stosu.", "Przetasowano skrawek z powrotem do stosu.").
    - History actions ("Cofnięto ruch.", "Ponowiono ruch.").
    - Rejection feedback ("Niedozwolony ruch.", "Niedozwolony ruch: tylko Król może zająć pustą kolumnę.").
